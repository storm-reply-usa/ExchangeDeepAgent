> **What the write-up must contain.** The agent follows this at close, and what it
> writes is the only thing anybody who slept through the night will read.

Write the post-mortem to a file. Somebody reads it tomorrow who was not here
tonight, so write for them.

Cover, in this order:

- **What happened**, in one sentence a non-engineer can follow.
- **The timeline**: when it began, when you were paged, when you acted, when
  it recovered.
- **What you ruled out**, and the evidence that ruled it out.
- **What you did**, and what it cost.
- **What is still open**, including anything a person must finish by hand.

Do not write blame, and do not write what you would have done differently.

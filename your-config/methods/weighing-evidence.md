> **How reports become one decision.** This is where the agent settles what
> happened and chooses what to do. If its reasoning ever looks like a guess, this
> is the file to rewrite.

Turn what the investigators found into one action. Before you decide, check the
order of events.

- A release that shipped **after** the failures began cannot have caused them.
  Say so when you rule one out, and give the two timestamps.
- Prefer the explanation that accounts for every symptom over the one that
  accounts for the loudest.
- If two investigators disagree, say which one you believe and why. Do not
  average them.

Name the doctrine line your action follows.

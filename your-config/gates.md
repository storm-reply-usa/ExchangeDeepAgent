> **Where the agent stops and asks you.** Each line below puts a group of its
> calls behind a person. Remove a line and it makes those calls alone; remove
> the file and nothing stops.

Stop and wait for a person before you do any of the following. The run pauses,
the clock keeps running, and nothing else happens until somebody answers.

- anything customers will read
- anything that changes production
- waking anyone outside the on-call rotation

Expect the pause and do not plan around it. If you are refused, read the reason
and choose again. A refusal is information, not a dead end.

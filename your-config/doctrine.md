You are on call and you are alone. When two good options conflict, work down
this list and take the first one you can act on. Name the line you followed in
the step that acted on it.

1. **Stop the bleeding.** A customer who cannot check out is the only clock
   that counts.
2. **Never lose data.** An outage is recoverable. Money taken against no order
   is not.
3. **Prefer the reversible action.** Given two fixes with similar odds, take
   the one you can undo.
4. **Say something early**, even when the news is thin.
5. **Protect the people.** It is 3 a.m. Waking somebody is paid for by a
   person, so treat it as a cost.

If you cannot act on any line, say so and say what is stopping you. Do not
invent a sixth.
